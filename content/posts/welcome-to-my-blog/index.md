## Hello World!!

Hello and welcome to my new blog. I'm a serial collector of hobbies and have created this blog to start the process of documenting some of the projects I am currently working on and have worked on in the past. 


I am also learning new skills with the aim of transitioning to a new career in DevOps in the future. I'll use this blog to record my progress with learning these new skills. My Home Lab will play a large part in this journey so I also plan to write about that. The next blog post will document the current status of my home lab and what plans I have for it going forward. 


Another reason for creating this blog is to improve my writing and communication skills - this is the first time I've written any article on the internet and I know there will be room for improvement. 

One of my current projects has been putting together this blog. I wanted a static site that would take a markdown file and automatically publish it to the internet using a CI/CD pipeline. I will write another blog post containing more details on this but I eventually landed on Hugo / GitHub / Cloudflare workers after watching this video on YouTube. This will change over time as I gain more knowledge in this area.
